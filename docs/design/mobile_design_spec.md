# EasyTax Mobile App Design Specification (Matching easytax.live)

## 1. Official easytax.live Theme & Tokens
- **Brand Green (Primary)**: `#3CB944` (Vibrant tax savings, success & primary highlights)
- **Brand Green 2 (Secondary Teal)**: `#1D9E75`
- **Brand Navy (Deep Authority)**: `#1C3557` (Primary cards, headings, and logo text)
- **Highlight Green**: `#28AA4A`
- **Background Wash**: `#F7FAF9` (Soft mint-white ambient canvas)
- **Pure White**: `#FFFFFF` (Secondary content cards)
- **Text Body**: `#45464F`
- **Typography**: **Manrope** (Google Fonts / Figma built-in)
- **Border Radius**: `16px` (Cards), `9999px` (Pills & badges)

## 2. Canvas Dimensions
- Frame Size: `393 × 852 px` (iPhone 15/16 Pro / Android Large)
- Horizontal Margin: `16px`
- Card Corner Radius: `16px`
